1. Register the property in GSC Wizard.
2. Submit https://alborzframe.ir/sitemap.xml
3. Track homepage, /karaj/, /services/ and core service URLs.
4. Inspect the highest-priority money pages after deployment.

## Google Business Profile fields pending real business data
Do not publish fabricated NAP data. Required before profile/schema completion:
- Exact real business name
- Real phone number
- Workshop/customer-facing address OR service-area-only configuration
- Opening hours
- Primary category and secondary categories
- Real service areas
- Real project photos
- Real customer reviews only

## GBP/site consistency rule
When the real business information is supplied, use the exact same business name, phone, website URL, service area and opening-hours conventions across Google Business Profile and the website. Add telephone/address/openingHours to LocalBusiness schema only after those real values are confirmed.
